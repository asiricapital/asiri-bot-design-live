# ASIRI Research Intelligence v2

This isolated preview upgrades the Vane candidate into an evidence-first research command center.

## Added in v2
- Compact mobile-first hero so the research workspace appears earlier on iPhone.
- Research templates: Catalyst Scan, Risk Scan, Compare, Pre-market Brief.
- Speed / Balanced / Quality modes and source filters.
- Live market-context lookup for stock symbols referenced in a research query.
- Evidence Ledger with provenance and evidence-gap semantics.
- Research output fields for Catalyst, Risk, Confidence and Evidence Gap.
- Local recent-research history in the browser.
- Research Playbooks for repeatable market investigation workflows.
- Explicit governance: research-only, no broker, no portfolio writes, no automatic BUY/SELL.

## Next integration boundary
Vane/SearxNG remains disconnected until the candidate passes review. When connected, external claims must return source URL, publication time, source class and evidence score before they can raise confidence.
